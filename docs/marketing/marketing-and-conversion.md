# ElderIndex marketing and conversion operating framework
Version 1.0 | 27 October 2026. Scope is credible UK care-sector agency marketing, not client care operations consulting.

## Objectives
1. Win relevant WEBSITE DESIGN/REDESIGN enquiries (highest commercial priority).
2. Convert appropriately into OPTIONAL website management/maintenance.
3. Win separately scoped care SEO/local/directory projects where useful.
All channels and pages should make it clear ElderIndex is a specialist digital agency and not a care placement service. Qualified enquiries matter more than undifferentiated traffic.

## User journeys (not compulsory funnels)
TOFU: guides that directly solve operator problems and establish credible expertise. MOFU: why agency experience matters, audience pages, process, service comparisons and honest project scope. BOFU: design, management, specialist SEO and directory service pages with immediate contact; no guide must be read first. Visitors may discover ElderIndex through organic search, referrals, sector relationships, listings, professional recommendations or social. Map real next questions instead of pushing every visitor towards an identical CTA.

## Commercial clarity standards
Every service page should state who it is for, situation/problem, deliverables in broad truthfully supportable terms, process, owner inputs and approvals, ownership/handover where applicable, explicit exclusions/limitations, direct enquiry, and optional adjacent service only when relevant. Management is not compulsory after build. An SEO retainer must not be represented as part of ordinary website maintenance. Don't fabricate pricing, turnaround times, SLAs, design portfolios, existing client success or partner arrangements. Named individual enquiry buttons may route to a shared monitored inbox if truthfully described.

## Conversion and CRO
Avoid manipulative urgency, popups, exaggerated performance promises or asking for sensitive resident medical information. Test CTAs, navigation, forms, responsive layouts, accessibility, actual receiver delivery and error handling. Distinguish contact_click from accepted_form and qualified_lead. Use actual user evidence before large redesigns. Do not trade trust for shallow click metrics.

## Brand, social and campaign communication
Professional web agency, not 'dog walker' softness or hospital-corporate sterility. Use restrained graphic motifs, readable typography, accurate real/illustrative imagery with disclosure, and original operational insights. Social posts can repurpose site guides or founder experience, but may not invent outcomes or pass off stock as clients. Paid media, cold outreach, scraping/sending email and new directory accounts require owner authorisation; Codex can research and prepare an ethical draft and measurement proposal. Spend limit is ZERO unless explicitly set by owner.

## Targetable channels
Organic Google and other search: SEO operating standard. Business/profile/directory presence: verified ownership, registration and eligibility; no fake addresses. Digital PR: credible earned placements, fact-checked references and outreach approved before sending. Referral partners: only verified relationships, no implied endorsements. Analytics: consent-aware; never log medical/resident details.

## Quality threshold
Before publishing any marketing asset, check factual basis, commercial audience, truthful proof, unique value, legal/privacy impact, spelling, accessibility, correct links, attribution, next action and change record. New channel strategy requires owner decision; established low-risk corrections follow policy without micro-approval.
