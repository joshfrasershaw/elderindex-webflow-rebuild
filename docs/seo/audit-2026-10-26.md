# Rendered-site internal-link audit — 26 October 2026

Status: reviewed by the owner and explicitly approved for publication to `main`; Cloudflare completion remains unverified. Baseline commit: `b405cd0`. Scope: all 22 generated canonical routes and the 404 utility template, not a sample. This is a source/build audit, not a measurement of live rankings or conversions.

## Results

- 22 canonical pages reviewed, plus the 404 utility page with its existing return-to-home link; no new pages or URL changes.
- 24 contextual links added across 14 existing pages.
- Zero existing links removed, including anchor text and placement classification.
- All captured titles, descriptions, canonicals and H1/H2 headings remain identical.
- No broken internal destinations, orphan pages or pages unreachable from the homepage.
- No repeated source/destination contextual pairs before or after this change.
- Existing redirects, sitemap membership, legal draft/noindex policy and direct enquiry CTAs are retained.
- The five guides with changed prose receive a genuine 26 October modification date. Their publication dates and the untouched website guide's dates remain unchanged.
- The two legal pages without main-body inbound links are `/terms/` and `/cookies/`; footer discovery is intentional.

## Journey review

- A direct Care Home SEO or Home Care SEO visitor still has an immediate SEO enquiry route. A reader facing site-structure limitations can instead explore Design, optional Management and then an enquiry.
- A direct Management visitor can enquire about maintenance, or compare Design only where platform limitations justify it.
- A local SEO guide reader can still reach Home Care SEO, while choosing Management for ongoing coverage updates.
- Directory and profile readers retain directory/SEO routes. A poor destination website creates a contextual reason to consider Design; maintaining approved facts creates a reason to consider Management.
- Audience pages now connect their existing explanations of design, management, SEO and profiles to the appropriate services.
- Guides answer their questions and retain their specific commercial next steps. No compulsory TOFU → MOFU → BOFU sequence is introduced.

## Reproduction and limitations

The baseline and full comparison JSON reports are available in this execution workspace at `/tmp/elderindex-links-before.json` and `/tmp/elderindex-links-after.json`. Run `npm run build`, then `npm run audit:links -- --output <path>` for a new report; use `--compare <baseline>` for removed-link/metadata differences. Full commands and future audit rules are in `internal-linking.md`.

No Search Console or live ranking data was provided. Preserving page intent, metadata and established links reduces unnecessary structural change; it cannot establish that rankings will remain unchanged. The owner has explicitly authorised publishing this reviewed change. Evaluate real search and enquiry performance after the deployment; a successful push alone does not verify Cloudflare completion.
