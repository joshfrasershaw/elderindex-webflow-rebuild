# Permanent SEO and commercial strategy

Owner's brief incorporated: 26 October 2026. Apply this document in every future Codex SEO audit, content recommendation and internal-linking review. The repository's `AGENTS.md` makes it part of the standard audit instructions.

## Commercial objectives, in priority order

1. Website design and redesign for care homes and home care agencies.
2. Ongoing website management and maintenance.
3. SEO, local search visibility and directory optimisation.

Design and management deserve prominent, relevant enquiry routes. This priority is not a reason to replace SEO-focused pages with design sales copy, remove valuable SEO links, or redirect informational visitors away from the answer they searched for. SEO services and useful guides remain acquisition routes and legitimate services in their own right.

Keep the existing architecture unless evidence and an approved scope justify a change. The shared Website Design URL already serves both audiences: use care-home or home-care-specific anchors where appropriate rather than inventing separate service URLs. One page can satisfy related phrases without creating another page for each keyword variant.

A visitor can land directly on any service, audience page or guide. Commercial pages need an immediate enquiry option; they should not require a guide visit first. Guides should answer the question before offering a relevant implementation or maintenance option. Audience and hub pages should help visitors compare services without implying a mandatory bundle.

## Protect the acquisition foundation

- Treat current titles, meta descriptions, H1/H2 structure, URLs, canonicals, redirects and valuable internal links as a baseline to inspect before editing. Any justified change needs an explicit reason and review.
- Keep SEO service pages focused on their primary searches and operational audience. Cross-selling supplements the answer; it does not become a competing primary target.
- Preserve useful guide-to-SEO and SEO-to-guide relationships, even when adding a design or management route.
- Do not automatically recommend a redesign. Explain when an existing website can support the required improvements and when a limitation may justify design work.
- Keep management optional and scoped; do not imply it is compulsory after a build, automatically includes directory/SEO work, or guarantees a technical or commercial outcome.
- No invented rankings, traffic, occupancy results, testimonials, client projects or official platform partnerships. A code audit does not establish live ranking performance.
- Use Search Console queries, landing pages and enquiry records when authorised data is available. Do not fabricate that evidence or require it as a reason to leave obvious broken links unfixed.

## Content evidence and service clarity

Care-sector experience should change practical decisions: provider language, genuine care provision and coverage, approved photographs/people profiles, who receives an enquiry and who keeps facts current. Attribute Josh's observations as personal experience; do not turn them into market statistics or ElderIndex client results. The agency is new and has no completed client projects or results to claim. Clearly labelled concepts remain illustrations.

The existing combined Design page can explain single-home, group and home-care needs without splitting into keyword variants. Keep handover/self-management, optional website management and separately scoped SEO clear. Expand useful existing guides before creating new pages; validate a distinct audience question and substantive evidence first. Ambiguous phrases such as “care home websites” and “home care web management” require actual search-intent checks, not automatic exact-match targets.

Audit final rendered HTML after editorial overrides. Schema.org relationships must describe visible facts and appropriate page images; a shared social card is not automatically every page's primary image. A schema graph is not evidence of Google Knowledge Graph inclusion, ranking strength or rich-result eligibility. Record when competitor/source access or live search evidence is unavailable.

## Measurement and publication

Use placement-specific referral counts and customer-journey checks as diagnostics, not link quotas or ranking forecasts. Where real analytics is configured and consent allows, review successful enquiries for design, management and SEO separately, alongside the provider's own enquiry records. Never send enquiry contents or personal data as analytics event parameters.
