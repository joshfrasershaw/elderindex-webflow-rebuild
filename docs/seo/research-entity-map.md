# Search research, intent ownership and entity/topic architecture
Version 1.0 | 27 October 2026. Baseline hypothesis, not live volume/ranking evidence.

## Method
Gather UK SERP patterns from currently accessible public evidence, first-party Search Console where authorised, related queries, competitor page type and person/provider search intent. Record country, device, exact date, source URLs, observed query/intent and uncertainty. Search volumes and keyword difficulty are estimates if supplied by reputable tools; never invent them. Distinguish user who is looking for a CARE HOME from user looking for a company that DESIGNS care home websites. Do not let Codex treat homonyms as proven keywords.

For each proposed URL designate 1 primary intent, supportive related intents, stage (information/comparison/transaction), semantic entities, proof requirements, internal-link parents/children, allowed CTAs, competing local page and author/owner. Audit rendered headings and links and compare current GSC queries to intent. Evidence for splitting a page: persistently distinct buyer intents + divergent SERPs + substantive unique deliverables + manageable internal links; do NOT split just to capture keyword variants.

## Topic clusters
Commercial core: Design <-> commissioning guide <-> optional Management <-> optional Search. Care Home SEO <-> directory comparison <-> Carehome.co.uk profile + GBP as appropriate. Home Care SEO <-> basic local SEO guide <-> multi-location guide. Directory Management <-> profiles and platform guide. Each cross-link must answer a real next question, not game PageRank. Owners/teams are the readers, not residents searching for placements; prevent patient-facing or service-finder confusion.

## Entity relationships and validation
ElderIndex organisation -> Josh (actual founder) -> genuine services -> canonical WebPage -> Article where applicable; shared stable @id and truthful visible attributes. Do not assert Google Knowledge Graph membership. Do not add business location or LocalBusiness schema without actual, appropriate, publicly approved location. Company may cover UK but geographic reach does not imply many offices. Validate author credibility, meaningful topic coverage, correct schema and on-page text. A dedicated topic graph diagram is optional; the mapped relationships and source of truth are mandatory.

## New content decision test
What distinct user problem? Which owned page already answers? What first-hand experience or primary source makes new content valuable? Does it resolve a topic gap instead of cannibalising? What is the internal-link plan and review owner? When will accuracy be checked? Proposed pages require editorial/architecture review under decision rights, even if Codex writes full draft itself.
