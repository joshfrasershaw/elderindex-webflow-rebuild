# Editorial, evidence and care-sector claims policy
Version 1.0 | 27 October 2026.

## Voice and audience
ElderIndex is a specialist digital agency serving UK residential/nursing care homes and domiciliary/live-in providers. Buyers are owners, nominated individuals, operations directors, registered managers and decision makers. Sound like a credible professional web agency with genuine insight into occupancy, enquiries, referrals, capacity and operational pressures. Warm, clear and commercially aware, not childish, sentimental, highly corporate or aggressively sales-driven. British English. Avoid unnecessary eyebrow labels, badges, buzzword-heavy promises and stock AI marketing clichés.

Attribute lessons as Josh's own experience, NOT measured market statistics. Public identity is first-name 'Josh' unless the owner approves additional disclosure. ## What care operators actually need
Families may discover providers through social workers, referrals, directories and people they trust, then consult website to validate. Websites should support a calm, authentic, helpful first conversation, not replace it. Explain real spaces, people, activities, practical care provision and who responds to enquiries. Distinguish funding routes, care fit and capacity without inventing eligibility; providers approve claims of services, fees, opening times, care capacity, packages and assessments. Understand care-home group versus single home versus home-care office/coverage. Domiciliary operating area is not an office/regulated branch. Recruitment enquiries should not be conflated with family care enquiries.

## Claim and source hierarchy
1. Owner/client-approved first-party operational facts for their own provision and commitments.
2. Current, primary regulator/official platform guidance for legal/regulatory eligibility, technical SEO and directory functions (e.g. CQC, relevant UK regulators, Google Search Central, ICO, official directory docs).
3. Clearly attributable independent sources for comparative patterns, with observed date and link.
4. Practitioner perspectives explicitly labelled as such.
5. Competitor marketing only to understand positioning; never copy prose, hallucinate prices or accept promises as fact.

When unable to access sources, record 'unverified'; don't recycle dated numbers as current. Significant objective claims must have a verification record. Compliance, medical, fees and legal statements require owner/provider/qualified approval before publication. Content can explain processes without dispensing clinical advice or claiming entitlement.

## Photography, permissions and demonstrations
Prefer genuine authorised staff/premises photos when available, but an honest project can launch using suitable illustrative stock. Never imply stock subjects are actual clients/residents. Permission and rights records precede publication, especially for identifiable residents, people lacking capacity and safeguarding concerns. Do not alter real faces or settings deceptively to simulate client work. Publish only approved people profiles and voluntary biographical details; respect UK GDPR and provider privacy policies. Optional independent photo/video referral is NOT an in-house capability.

## Copy structure and SEO
Content must solve buyer intent, explain value and deliverables, limitations, handover and independently optional services. Straightforward title/H1, meaningful H2s, useful examples, real FAQs if helpful, contextual service links, immediate enquiry route on commercial pages. Avoid automated padding, repeated generic content, arbitrary word counts, unsupported CRO claims, false urgency, forced mandatory retainers and unrequested redesign pitches.

## Workflow and human review
Codex may research and draft/revise within approved policy, test rendering and source validity and submit a review package. Changes affecting brand positioning, policy/fees, regulated assertions, testimonials/credentials, partner commitments or major claims need explicit human review before live publication. Technical typos/clearly verified factual corrections in the approved deterministic class can use delegated authority per decision-rights.md. Maintain truthful publication and update dates; no cosmetic date refresh.
