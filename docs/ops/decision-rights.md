# Delegated authority and exception handling
Version 1.0 | 27 October 2026 | Applies to Codex and any other AI modifying ElderIndex.

## Principle
Correct the actual state to the approved state. The framework, not a guess that something is a fact, determines authority. A noindex on staging is correct; a noindex on an approved, live commercial URL is not. Distinguish environment, intention, reversibility, reach and risk.

## A: ACT autonomously (no micro-approval)
Codex may research, crawl, propose, write, test and commit framework-compliant work directly to main as authorised in autonomous-publishing.md. It may correct deterministic, low-risk violations of already-approved requirements: broken internal links with a unique verified target, accidental heading/meta omissions, duplicate IDs, invalid schema referencing non-existent objects, image alt text that is clearly erroneous, test regressions, build failures, harmless factual typos corroborated by primary evidence. Revalidate all page dependencies. Document every material change, including source and intended effect.

The owner has now explicitly enabled framework-led autonomous publishing, including ordinary editorial and SEO improvements. Run relevant checks; push to main; confirm actual deployed state; record before/after and rollback. Before the first domain switch, don't alter live DNS or hosting bindings. Do not self-certify legal/regulated claims or account actions.

## B: EXECUTE and PUBLISH approved strategy autonomously
Within the SEO and editorial playbooks, Codex may autonomously research, refine and publish articles, useful content, links, confirmed technical guidance, metadata, performance and changes consistent with the already approved SEO/editorial/brand framework. Preserve commercial priority and link/URL ownership. Core strategy/policy, genuine business commitments, new unverified personal/regulated assertions and irreversible/high-impact URL deletion still require explicit owner authority. PRs are exceptional tools only for blocked access or serious conflicts. If there is no evidence for a separate search intent, improve the existing page rather than multiplying pages.

## C: ESCALATE specific exception before action
Requires Josh's explicit decision: initial domain/DNS cutover, host/canonical changes impacting the live site outside approved host rules, deletion of valuable URLs without an equivalent or evidence-based retirement plan, broad indexability changes outside approved page manifest, new product or price promises, client case studies, legal/regulatory conclusions, unverified clinical/funding claims, paid spending, outreach sent to third parties, account credentials, new external integrations, access policies, destructive operations, rollback of a production release, or choosing between contradictory factual sources.

Human/qualified review: legal/privacy and data-processing disclosures by business owner and appropriate adviser; clinical and care-eligibility assertions by appropriate qualified source/provider; pricing/contractual statements by business owner. No AI can self-certify those.

## Production and staging invariants
Staging and private previews: noindex; do not remove to 'improve SEO'. Production intended commercial/informational pages: indexable and accessible according to the approved release manifest; legal drafts intentionally noindex until reviewed; canonical host must match approved setting. Do not expose staging by changing SITE_ENV without gate approval. A live noindex violation AFTER a signed-off release is an eligible deterministic correction in the authorised deploy lane only.

## Escalation contract
Do not ask speculative questions. Investigate first, then give: (1) exact failed standard ID and evidence; (2) operational impact; (3) preferred remedy and alternatives; (4) what evidence/approval is missing; (5) safe interim action. If work is blocked, continue independent safe tasks and report blockers together.

## Prohibited independently
No invented ranking/traffic/client results; no falsified reviews, credentials, images or partnerships; no purchased/manipulative backlinks; no mass-generated thin pages; no personal/sensitive enquiry data in tracking; no bypassing auth; no publishing private CMS drafts; no secret values in code/log; no silent overwrites of changes in another branch; no unapproved automatic DNS switch.

## Acceptance
No task is 'done' unless it states its baseline, changed files/URLs, evidence, tests, state (draft/merged/deployed/live verified), and rollback instructions as appropriate.
